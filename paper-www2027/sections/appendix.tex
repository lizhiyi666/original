\section{Additional Implementation Settings}
\label{app:algo}
Algorithm~\ref{alg:pcdg} and \S\ref{sec:exp:setup} give the controlled step
and main sampling settings. Dual variables initialize at $1$,
penalties grow by $2$ up to $10^3$, and the violation threshold is $\tau=0$
with multiplier-update tolerance $10^{-6}$. Early stopping is disabled.
In the batch implementation, token KL sums over vocabulary and positions for
active sequences and divides by the full batch size; order/existence penalties
sum over active relations and sequences without that division.
Geometry uses distance/radius/fidelity weights $(1,2,0.1)$, $8$ paths, up to
$32$ candidates, temperature $1$, Adam rate $0.05$, $200$ steps, and noise
refresh every $20$ steps. The full-stack weights are $(\rho,\lambda,\beta)=(0.5,0,1)$.
Geometry's timing qualification is in Appendix~\ref{app:cost}.

\section{Distribution-Correction Grid and Selection}
\label{app:grid}
We screened
$\rho\in\{0.25,0.5,1\}$, $\lambda\in\{0,1\}$, and $\beta\in\{0,0.1,1\}$
on $512$ training-side conditions per city with one sampling seed.
Table~\ref{tab:grid} retains the archived \emph{v2} screening values.
Screening requires all five constraint metrics to be no worse than \pcdgp
in each city: strict OVR, skip OVR and Unsat must not increase, and pair/category
coverage must not decrease. Confirmation additionally requires each of the six
JSDs to be no greater than JointGen's. Comparisons use unrounded city-wise
sampling-seed means, an absolute tolerance of $10^{-12}$, and reject undefined
metrics. Confirmation uses a separate $512$-condition training-side pool per city;
the remaining training records form the distribution-correction reference pool.
The shared-city ranking minimizes the maximum, then mean, of twelve
JSD/JointGen ratios, followed by the parameter tuple to break ties.
Upstream samples are cached by city, calibration pool, seed, $\rho$, and
$\lambda$, so the three $\beta$ refinements share upstream randomness and
the grid uses a fixed candidate-pool construction.

The top two configurations, $(0.5,0,1)$ and $(0.5,0,0.1)$, underwent

Distance, Radius, and DailyLoc ceilings, but failed CategoryTransition,
Category, and G-RANK ceilings on calibration data. Thus the original

confirmation gates, and final candidate order are unchanged under v2.
For the selected $(0.5,0,1)$, the maximum/mean confirmation ratios are

$0.9103$ and $0.9135$, respectively. The selected configuration was frozen
before formal test sampling. Test metrics were used only for reporting:
eleven of eleven gates hold on Istanbul and nine on New York.
The calibration pool was seen during backbone training and is not an
independent validation set.

\section{Fidelity Metrics and Audit Details}
\label{app:metrics}
Section~\ref{sec:exp:metrics} defines the five catalog-category constraint metrics.
The separate diagnostic \texttt{token\_strict\_ovr} uses category tokens.
Mismatch rate pools the event-wise disagreements between tokens and catalog
categories.

\paragraph{JSD and filtering.}
For count vectors $P,Q$, the implementation forms
$p=P/\sum P+\epsilon$, $q=Q/\sum Q+\epsilon$, $\epsilon=10^{-14}$, without a
second normalization, and evaluates
$\frac12\sum_jp_j\log(p_j/m_j)+\frac12\sum_jq_j\log(q_j/m_j)$,
$m=(p+q)/2$, using natural logarithms. A zero-total vector is undefined.
Distance, Radius, DailyLoc, and G-RANK retain sequences with at least two GPS
points, independently on each side; the two category metrics include shorter
sequences. Distance additionally requires the aligned generated/reference
first and last categories to match. This subset can vary by method, so the
reported Distance is not an unconditional whole-population divergence.

\paragraph{Six fidelity statistics.}
Distance is cumulative consecutive-point Haversine distance (Earth radius
$6371$ km). With arithmetic latitude/longitude centroid $\bar x$, Radius is
\[
  \mathrm{Radius}=\sqrt{n^{-1}\sum_j d_{\mathrm{Hav}}(x_j,\bar x)}.
\]
Its unit is $\sqrt{\mathrm{km}}$ and it
is \emph{not} root-mean-square radius of gyration.
DailyLoc counts distinct POI identities per sequence.
For these three histograms, edges are exactly
$\operatorname{arange}(0,M,M/100)$, where $M$ is the larger observed maximum.
This usually produces $99$ bins, omits $M$, drops values beyond the last edge,
and renormalizes retained mass. If $M=0$, the divergence is $0$; a zero
histogram otherwise yields an undefined value. These historical conventions
are preserved for comparability, not proposed as new estimators.

Category averages category-frequency JSDs equally over the $24$ hours.
CategoryTransition is the equal-weight mean of conditional next-category
JSDs over source categories with any outgoing transition on either side;
self-transitions are included and sequence boundaries excluded. A row/hour
empty on both sides contributes $0$; one empty on only one side contributes
$\ln2$. No events or no transitions, respectively, gives an undefined metric.
G-RANK retains each side's top-$1000$ visited POIs (all if fewer), aligns
their union by POI identity with zeros for missing retained IDs, and computes
JSD after normalizing retained mass. It compares identities, not rank positions.

\paragraph{Deterministic audit.}
Evaluation v2 uses an unstable frequency sort at the top-$1000$ cutoff.
The v3 audit breaks ties by POI id; identity alignment is present in both
versions. Across the $18$ formal audit metric sets (six full-stack outputs
and twelve reused baseline sets), the largest absolute G-RANK change is
$0.0032$; other statistics agree within $10^{-12}$.
The full-stack/backbone G-RANK comparison remains $0.1766<0.1892$ on
Istanbul and $0.1912>0.1628$ on New York under v3.
This audit does not replace any v2 table cell.

\section{Cost}
\label{app:cost}
Table~\ref{tab:cost} reports the earlier selected-geometry qualification,
not the later full-stack timing. On each city, ordinary and long cases are
eligible $64$-sequence batches chosen by lower-median and maximum batchwise
generated length, respectively. Each case uses two warmups and five measured
repetitions, seed $135398$, FP32 with TF32 disabled, and the same CUDA device
and input. CUDA synchronization brackets the timer. Ratios divide the two
medians, rather than averaging per-trial ratios.
\input{tables/cost}

The timer covers spatial sampling and decoding, including fresh scoring,
candidate construction, optimization, and discrete selection when geometry
is enabled; it excludes temporal generation, model loading, and transfer.
The denominator is projection \textsc{Full} (\pcdgp), not JointGen.
The selected $200$-step configuration uses radius/fidelity weights $2/0.1$;
an earlier step-budget probe used $1/0.01$. All four selected-configuration
ratios are below the $1.10$ ceiling, with overhead $3.35$--$3.98\%$.
These measurements cannot be transferred to \pcdgpd plus geometry or the
final $(0.5,0,1)$ stack. Comparable full-stack/JointGen sampling overhead and
PO-CFG training GPU-hours remain unmeasured in the archived cost evidence.
