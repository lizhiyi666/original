\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Location-based social networks (LBSNs) record time-stamped visits to points of
interest (POIs). Such traces support POI recommendation~\cite{feng2018deepmove,luo2021stan,yang2022getnext},
but expose sensitive behavior. Synthetic mobility generation offers an
alternative data source~\cite{feng2020simulate,yuan2022activity,long2023synthetic,deng2025marionette}.

\paragraph{Relevance to the Web.}
We generate complete LBSN check-in sequences under activity-order requirements
while retaining population statistics. These traces could support recommender
stress tests and data augmentation without releasing individual logs. We evaluate
controllable generation; downstream utility and formal privacy protection remain
unevaluated.

A scenario may require leisure before dining or sightseeing before shopping.
Precedence couples non-adjacent positions: every occurrence of one category must
precede every occurrence of another, and both must appear. A local condition
embedding does not ensure this. Reordering fixes inversions but cannot supply
missing activities. Road-topology
control~\cite{zhu2024controltraj} addresses geographic structure, whereas constrained
discrete diffusion (CDD)~\cite{cardei2025cdd} provides a general framework for
sampling under differentiable constraints. POI generation adds a further
difficulty: category tokens and POI identities can disagree, so controlling the
former need not control the actual activities of the emitted POIs. The control
must therefore survive the mapping from category tokens to catalog locations.

We introduce \pcdg, a training-free control stack on the frozen Marionette
generator~\cite{deng2025marionette}. It adapts CDD's KL-anchored projection to
sample-level order and existence energies, with a distance-distribution
regularizer on POI logits. Category-consistent decoding then restricts POI support
using the sampled category, connecting token-level control to realized
activities. After diffusion, same-category geometry refinement changes POI
identities and coordinates while preserving actual categories, times, and event
order. This restricted substitution leaves the fields that determine constraint
satisfaction invariant, while allowing spatial statistics to change.

Across Istanbul and New York, the stack improves every constraint metric over
the retrained PO-CFG baseline. Geometry refinement lowers spatial divergences
without changing constraint metrics; the full stack improves all six fidelity
JSDs over the unconstrained backbone on Istanbul and four on New York. New York
CategoryTransition and G-RANK remain worse. These results identify a recoverable
spatial cost in this generator, rather than establishing that every
fidelity--control trade-off can be removed.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item We formulate activity-precedence control over actual POI categories and
  adapt CDD's projection to order/existence energies on a frozen Marionette
  backbone (\S\ref{sec:problem}--\S\ref{sec:method:project}).
  \item We connect category tokens to catalog POIs through support-constrained
  decoding, then refine spatial assignments within each realized category while
  preserving constraint-relevant fields (\S\ref{sec:method:decoding}--\S\ref{sec:method:geometry}).
  \item We use two-city, three-seed comparisons and projection ablations to
  identify a recoverable spatial cost of control, alongside unresolved category
  and popularity errors (\S\ref{sec:experiments}).
\end{itemize}
