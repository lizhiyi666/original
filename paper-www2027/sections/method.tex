\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\pcdg is a \emph{training-free} control layer that is attached to a frozen,
pre-trained conditional check-in generator and acts only during reverse-diffusion
sampling. We first recall the backbone generator (\S\ref{sec:method:backbone}),
then formalize the partial-order constraints as differentiable energies
(\S\ref{sec:method:energy}), describe the KL-preserving augmented-Lagrangian
projection (\S\ref{sec:method:project}) and the complementary distance
regularizer (\S\ref{sec:method:distance}), and finally specify where the control
is applied in the reverse process (\S\ref{sec:method:placement}).

\subsection{Backbone: conditional joint discrete diffusion}
\label{sec:method:backbone}
We build on a conditional generator that models arrival times with a diffusion
point process and the discrete content (categories and POIs) with a conditional
discrete diffusion model, following the fine-grained trajectory generator of
\citet{deng2025marionette}. The temporal branch samples event times; the discrete
branch represents a sequence as a token string
$\mathbf{x}=[\textsc{start},\,k_{1:n},\,\textsc{sep},\,l_{1:n},\,\textsc{end}]$,
with a \emph{category mask} and a \emph{POI mask} identifying the two segments.
Categories and POIs use separate absorbing-state transition
matrices~\cite{austin2021d3pm}; a Transformer denoiser with an
$\mathbf{x}_0$-parameterization predicts, at each reverse step, per-position
logits $\mathbf{z}\in\mathbb{R}^{V\times L}$ over the joint vocabulary of size $V$
across the $L$ positions, from which the posterior $p(\mathbf{x}_{s-1}\!\mid\!\mathbf{x}_s)$
and the next discrete state are sampled. \textbf{The backbone is used as-is and is
never retrained by \pcdg}; our contribution modifies only the per-step sampling
distribution $\mathbf{z}$.

\subsection{Partial-order constraints as differentiable energies}
\label{sec:method:energy}
Given the sample-level matrix $\Mmat$, we parse it into a set of pairwise
constraints $\{(a,b): \Mmat[a,b]>\tfrac12\}$. To make a constraint differentiable
in the logits, we relax the hard categorical choice at each position into a
distribution. With temperature $T$ and (optionally) Gumbel noise $\mathbf{g}_l$,
\begin{equation}
  \boldsymbol{\pi}_l = \mathrm{softmax}\big((\mathbf{z}_l + \mathbf{g}_l)/T\big)\in\Delta^{V-1},
  \qquad l=1,\dots,L .
\end{equation}
We restrict attention to category positions $\mathcal{P}_k$ (via the category
mask) and to the category slice of the vocabulary, writing
$\pi^{\mathrm{cat}}_l(a)$ for the relaxed probability that position
$l\in\mathcal{P}_k$ takes category $a\in\mathcal{C}$. For a constraint $(a,b)$ let
$P^A_l=\pi^{\mathrm{cat}}_l(a)$ and $P^B_l=\pi^{\mathrm{cat}}_l(b)$. We define two
energies:
\begin{align}
  g^{\mathrm{ord}}_{(a,b)} &= \sum_{l\in\mathcal{P}_k}
     \Big(\textstyle\sum_{l'<l} P^B_{l'}\Big)\, P^A_l,
     \label{eq:order}\\[2pt]
  g^{\mathrm{ex}}_{(a,b)}  &= \relu\!\Big(1-\textstyle\sum_{l} P^A_l\Big)
                           + \relu\!\Big(1-\textstyle\sum_{l} P^B_l\Big).
     \label{eq:exist}
\end{align}
The \emph{order energy}~\eqref{eq:order} is the expected number of inverted pairs,
i.e.\ the probability mass of a $b$ appearing \emph{before} an $a$; it is zero iff
no $b$ precedes any $a$. The \emph{existence energy}~\eqref{eq:exist} penalizes an
expected count below one for either constrained category, which prevents the model
from trivially satisfying ordering by \emph{omitting} $a$ or $b$. Both energies are
computed only over category positions; POI logits do not enter
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:order}--\eqref{eq:exist}.

\subsection{KL-preserving augmented-Lagrangian projection}
\label{sec:method:project}
At a controlled reverse step, we project the backbone logits to reduce constraint
violations while staying close to the model's own distribution. Let
$\mathbf{y}^{(0)}=\mathbf{z}$ be the detached backbone logits and $\mathbf{y}$ a
trainable copy. We solve, per sequence,
\begin{equation}
  \min_{\mathbf{y}}\; \dkl\!\big(\mathrm{softmax}(\mathbf{y})\,\big\|\,\mathrm{softmax}(\mathbf{y}^{(0)})\big)
  \quad\text{s.t.}\quad g^{\mathrm{ord}}_{(a,b)}\le\tau,\; g^{\mathrm{ex}}_{(a,b)}\le\tau\;\;\forall(a,b),
  \label{eq:constrained}
\end{equation}
where the KL term anchors the projected distribution to the base model so that
fidelity is preserved away from the constrained categories. We optimize the
augmented-Lagrangian relaxation of~\eqref{eq:constrained}: with
$\delta^{\bullet}=\relu(g^{\bullet}-\tau)$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{L}(\mathbf{y}) =\; & w_{\mathrm{kl}}\,\dkl
   + \!\!\sum_{(a,b)}\! w_{\mathrm{ord}}\Big(\lambda^{\mathrm{ord}}\delta^{\mathrm{ord}}
     + \tfrac{1}{2}\mu^{\mathrm{ord}}(\delta^{\mathrm{ord}})^2\Big)\\
   & + \!\!\sum_{(a,b)}\! w_{\mathrm{ex}}\Big(\lambda^{\mathrm{ex}}\delta^{\mathrm{ex}}
     + \tfrac{1}{2}\mu^{\mathrm{ex}}(\delta^{\mathrm{ex}})^2\Big).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:auglag}
\end{equation}
An \emph{inner} loop takes projected-gradient steps on $\mathbf{y}$ (SGD with
learning rate $\eta$, gradient-norm clipping, updates restricted to positions of
constrained sequences). An \emph{outer} loop evaluates the \emph{hard} violations
$\hat{\delta}$ (from the $\arg\max$ one-hot assignment, not the relaxation) and
updates the dual variables,
\begin{equation}
  \lambda \leftarrow \lambda + \mu\,\hat{\delta},\qquad
  \mu \leftarrow \min(\alpha\,\mu,\;\mu_{\max})\ \text{if}\ \hat{\delta}>\tau_{\mathrm{tol}},
  \label{eq:dual}
\end{equation}
stopping early once $\max\hat{\delta}<\tau_{\mathrm{tol}}$. Evaluating the
multiplier update on hard violations lets the penalty grow precisely for the
sequences and constraints that remain unsatisfied, rather than applying a uniform
fixed penalty. Crucially, the projection reweights the sampling distribution
over category positions only; it performs \emph{no} POI candidate filtering and
imposes \emph{no} hard category$\rightarrow$POI consistency mask.

\subsection{Distance-distribution regularization}
\label{sec:method:distance}
Enforcing category order perturbs the category marginals and, indirectly, the
induced spatial statistics. To preserve geographic realism we add an independent
distance regularizer that matches the \emph{population} distribution of travel
length. From the training split only, we compute the cumulative great-circle
(Haversine) route length $d$ of every training sequence with at least two POIs and
summarize it as a soft histogram $H_{\mathrm{train}}$ over $\log(1+d)$ with fixed
Gaussian bins (the reference is content-hashed and cached, never stored in the
model). During projection, for each POI position we fix the top-$\kappa$ candidate
POIs under the backbone and draw $P$ routes with a straight-through
Gumbel-softmax (exact distance on the forward pass, a bilinear surrogate on the
backward pass), forming a differentiable route-length histogram
$H_{\mathrm{proj}}(\mathbf{y})$. We add the term
\begin{equation}
  w_{\mathrm{dist}}\,\dkl\!\big(H_{\mathrm{train}}\,\|\,H_{\mathrm{proj}}(\mathbf{y})\big)
  \label{eq:distance}
\end{equation}
to the objective~\eqref{eq:auglag}. Unlike the order/existence energies, this term
acts on \emph{POI-position} logits, and it is a batch-level distribution-matching
regularizer rather than a per-sample hard constraint; it is applied only to
sequences with at least two POIs and uses an independent random stream. Its weight
is not tuned per city and is not guaranteed to improve every fidelity metric; we
quantify its effect via the \textsc{No Distance KL} ablation
(\S\ref{sec:experiments}).

\subsection{Placement in the reverse process and joint sampling}
\label{sec:method:placement}
The control is applied in the \emph{late} reverse-diffusion steps---where the
predicted categories are informative enough for the energies to be
meaningful---specifically during the last $K$ steps and every $f$ steps therein.
At each such step, \pcdg replaces the backbone category-position logits with their
projected values (and, when enabled, the distance-adjusted POI-position logits),
and the next discrete state is obtained by the backbone's ordinary joint
categorical sampling over the full token sequence. The temporal branch is
unchanged. Because every operation reads and rewrites only the per-step sampling
logits of a frozen model, \pcdg adds no trainable parameters and requires no
retraining, and it reduces to the unconstrained backbone when the projection is
disabled. Algorithm pseudocode and default hyper-parameters are given in
