\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\noindent\textbf{Human mobility and POI modeling.}
Human movement is regular but boundedly predictable~\cite{gonzalez2008mobility,song2010predictability}.
Existing attentional, spatio-temporal, graph, and diffusion models mainly
predict the next location or recommendation~\cite{feng2018deepmove,luo2021stan,yang2022getnext,long2024cloudedge,wang2024dsdrec,chen2025sequentialdiffusion,qin2023diffpoi,wang2025gnprsid},
whereas we generate complete time-stamped visits with sample-level order rules.

\noindent\textbf{Synthetic trajectory and mobility generation.}
Prior work uses GAN/Seq2Seq, point-process, neural-ODE, imitation, privacy,
diffusion, and Transformer/LLM generators~\cite{cao2021trajgen,jiang2023tstrajgen,xiong2024trajsgan,lin2023mbpgail,yuan2022activity,long2023synthetic,feng2020simulate,wang2023pategail,sun2023sprt,rao2023cats,hu2024retrasyn,netzler2024privacy,zhu2023difftraj,yuan2023stdpp,gong2025stcdm,chen2024trajgdm,wei2024diffrntraj,rao2025seed,long2026population,zhang2024mobglm,hsu2024trajgpt,li2025geollama,haydari2026mobilitygpt}.
Marionette provides fine-grained global and partial contexts~\cite{deng2025marionette};
we freeze its generator and study category relations and their POI-distribution effects.

\noindent\textbf{Discrete diffusion.}
Discrete diffusion extends denoising diffusion to categorical data through
structured transitions, score matching, score-entropy, and masked formulations
~\cite{ho2020ddpm,austin2021d3pm,meng2022concrete,lou2024sedd,sahoo2024mdlm};
classifier-free and classifier guidance steer conditions~\cite{schiff2025guidance}.
EnergyGuide is our local-energy gradient baseline, while \pcdg intervenes in
the frozen backbone at sampling time.

\noindent\textbf{Sampling-time constraint projection.}
CDD integrates differentiable constraints into discrete-diffusion sampling
through KL anchoring and augmented-Lagrangian optimization without
retraining~\cite{cardei2025cdd}. PCDG derives its own cross-position order and
existence energies under the same general sampling-time projection idea. Its KL
anchor penalizes token-level drift from the denoiser; matching aggregate travel
and visitation statistics to training references is a separate objective,
evaluated by projection and stage-wise comparisons.

\noindent\textbf{Decoding support and\\[-1pt] spatial refinement.}
Grammar-constrained decoding and Control-DAG restrict formal outputs with
grammars or weighted automata~\cite{geng2023grammar,chen2024controldag}; our
decoder couples category tokens to admissible catalog POIs, with a fallback.
ControlTraj conditions on road topology and trip attributes~\cite{zhu2024controltraj}.
Our terminal refinement instead preserves the realized activity sequence while
reselecting same-category locations, and the stage-wise results quantify the
fidelity recovered under that restriction.
