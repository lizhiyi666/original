\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
With two cities and three seeds, we do not estimate training variability or
significance; backbone batch sizes differ and training provenance is incomplete.
PO-CFG scale $1$ is untuned, and batch-level distance KL gives no per-sequence
guarantee. Finite-budget projection and decoder fallback may leave violations;
same-category refinement cannot repair category-sequence errors, including
New York's transition gap. Calibration is not independent validation; failed
gates and the override are detailed in \S\ref{sec:exp:tradeoff} and
Appendix~\ref{app:grid}. Stage (d)$\to$(e) changes three factors jointly.
Complex DAGs, conflict handling, adaptive scheduling, training-time losses,
recommendation utility, and formal privacy remain open; distributional
similarity alone does not establish safe release.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
On a frozen generator, \pcdg adds projection, category-consistent decoding, and
same-category refinement for new order requests without retraining. It beats
PO-CFG on all five constraint measures in both cities and JointGen on six
fidelity divergences in Istanbul and four in New York. Refinement restores
spatial fidelity but leaves New York's transition and popularity errors.
